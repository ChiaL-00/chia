About

A decade quietly improving the boring, important parts of software.

I'm a product designer based in London. I've spent ten years working on the kind of software people don't choose to use — they have to.

That constraint is what I find interesting. When the user can't walk away, every misstep compounds. Good design here isn't about delight; it's about reducing friction in jobs people are paid to do, and giving them a tool that respects their attention.

Most of my work has been research-led: contextual interviews, ride-alongs, shift observations. I prefer to design from evidence rather than instinct, and I'm happiest in cross-functional teams where engineering and ops have a real voice in the room.
