I'm a UX/UI designer with experience designing complex digital products — from mobile apps used on the road to enterprise tools and consumer platforms. My work is grounded in research: I believe the most impactful design decisions start with understanding real people in real contexts.

I'm drawn to problems where the stakes are genuine — where thoughtful design can reduce stress, build confidence, and help people do their jobs better. I've worked across transport, fintech, and retail, collaborating with product managers, engineers, and business stakeholders to ship work that balances user needs with commercial goals.

My process is deliberate: I map the problem space before touching Figma, test early and often, and push for the simplest solution that solves the right problem. I care about quality at every level — from the hierarchy of a screen to the wording of an error message.

When I'm not designing, I'm probably reading about cognitive psychology, typography, or urban planning — the kinds of things that quietly inform how I think about interfaces.
